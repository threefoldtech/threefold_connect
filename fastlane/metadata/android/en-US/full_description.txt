ThreeFold Connect is a secure authentication and digital wallet application that allows you to:

• Login to ThreeFold services and applications with secure authentication
• Manage your digital TFT (ThreeFold Token) wallet
• Create and restore wallets with easy backup options
• Send and receive ThreeFold Tokens
• Monitor your ThreeFold farming rewards and nodes
• Validate your identity with 3Bot Connect
• Access the ThreeFold ecosystem securely
• Keep track of transactions and wallet activity

ThreeFold Connect provides a secure and user-friendly interface for interacting with the ThreeFold Grid, a decentralized internet infrastructure powered by independent farmers worldwide.
